# Word sets — 5 pictures

One prompt = one picture with 12 objects. Send each picture as a FILE.

## At breakfast — breakfast

```
Create ONE image: a 3x4 grid (3 columns, 4 rows) of 12 square panels with thin white gutters.
Each panel shows ONE object alone, centred on a plain soft warm-cream background, with a soft shadow under it.
STYLE: warm, detailed children's storybook illustration - soft gouache and coloured-pencil texture, gentle light, rich cosy colours, the same style and lighting in all 12 panels.
No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.
Order, left to right, top to bottom:
1. a round loaf of brown bread on a wooden board
2. a block of yellow butter on a small dish with a knife
3. a wedge of white and yellow cheese
4. a boiled egg in a blue egg cup
5. a glass of milk
6. a cup of tea with steam on a saucer
7. a jar of golden honey with a wooden honey dipper
8. a jar of red strawberry jam
9. a bowl of warm porridge with berries
10. two slices of golden toast
11. a glass of orange juice
12. a small bowl of white yoghurt with a spoon
```
