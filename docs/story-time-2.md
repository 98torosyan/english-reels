## 6. The kitten in the boot — st-boot
```
Create ONE image: a 3x2 grid of six square panels with thin white gutters.
STYLE: warm, detailed children's storybook illustration, like a classic picture book - soft gouache and coloured-pencil texture, cosy winter-morning light, warm funny mood.
DAD: a man of about 38 with short black hair, a short beard, a dark-green coat over a light-blue shirt, grey trousers.
PUMPKIN: a tiny fluffy ginger kitten with big green eyes.
THE FAMILY: a girl of 8 with a brown ponytail in yellow pyjamas and a mum with curly dark hair in a red jumper.
The same people and the same kitten in every panel. No text, letters or logos anywhere.
1. A busy hallway in the morning: Dad hurries, putting on his dark-green coat, looking at his watch.
2. Dad sits on a little bench and tries to put his foot into a brown winter boot; he looks puzzled.
3. Dad jumps back in surprise, the boot on the floor, a little "meow" moment, funny.
4. Close-up: inside the dark boot, two big green kitten eyes shine.
5. Dad lifts the sleepy ginger kitten out of the boot; she yawns.
6. The whole family laughs in the hallway; Dad holds the kitten, still late for work.
```

## 8. The giant pumpkin — st-pumpkin
```
Create ONE image: a 3x2 grid of six square panels with thin white gutters.
STYLE: warm, detailed children's storybook illustration, like a classic picture book - soft gouache and coloured-pencil texture, changing seasons in a village garden, warm golden colours.
VAHE: a 9-year-old boy with messy light-brown hair, freckles, a red checked shirt, blue dungarees and green wellies.
GRANDPA: a strong man of about 70 with a grey moustache, a straw hat and a beige shirt with rolled sleeves.
GRANDMA: a round, smiling woman of about 68 with a white headscarf and a flowered apron.
The same people in every panel. No text, letters or logos anywhere.
1. Spring: Vahe plants a pumpkin seed in Grandpa's garden and waters it with a small green watering can.
2. Summer: big green leaves and a growing orange pumpkin; Vahe kneels and smiles at it.
3. Autumn: the pumpkin is huge, taller than Vahe; he stands next to it, amazed.
4. Grandpa and Vahe push the giant pumpkin in a wooden wheelbarrow along a village road.
5. At a village fair with flags: the pumpkin wears a blue ribbon; Vahe holds up a little cup proudly.
6. Evening: Grandma serves bright orange pumpkin soup at a long table full of happy villagers.
```

## 10. Pancakes for Mum — st-pancakes
```
Create ONE image: a 3x2 grid of six square panels with thin white gutters.
STYLE: warm, detailed children's storybook illustration, like a classic picture book - soft gouache and coloured-pencil texture, early-morning golden kitchen light, funny happy mood.
ARSEN: a 10-year-old boy with short dark curly hair, a white chef's hat that is too big, a blue T-shirt and a striped apron.
MUM: a woman of about 37 with long brown hair, a soft pink dressing gown.
THE DOG: a fluffy white dog with brown ears.
The same people and the same dog in every panel. No text, letters or logos anywhere.
1. Early morning, still dark outside: Arsen tiptoes into the kitchen in his big chef's hat.
2. Arsen mixes eggs, milk and flour in a big bowl; flour on his nose.
3. Arsen flips a pancake high in the air from a frying pan like a real chef.
4. The pancake lands on the head of the fluffy white dog sitting by the stove.
5. The dog looks very happy, the pancake on its head like a hat, tail wagging; Arsen laughs.
6. Mum smiles at a table with a stack of pancakes, a small cake candle and a flower; Arsen hugs her.
```
