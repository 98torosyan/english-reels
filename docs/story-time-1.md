## 1. The kite on the hill — st-kite

```
Create ONE image: a 3x2 grid of six square panels with thin white gutters.
STYLE: warm, detailed children's storybook illustration, like a classic picture book - soft gouache and coloured-pencil texture, warm golden autumn light, gentle rich colours.

GRANDPA: a kind man of about 70 with white hair, a short white beard, a flat grey cap, a brown wool waistcoat over a white shirt.
The same Aram and the same Grandpa with the same faces and clothes in all six panels.
No text, letters or logos anywhere.
1. In a cosy wooden workshop, Aram and Grandpa make a big yellow kite with a long tail.
2. They walk up a grassy hill near a small Armenian village with stone houses, holding the kite.
3. Grandpa holds the string while Aram runs fast down the hill, the kite rising behind him.
4. The yellow kite flies high in a clear blue sky; Aram looks up and laughs.
5. The kite is caught in the branches of an old walnut tree; Aram looks sad.
6. Grandpa has got the kite back; the kite flies again and both of them smile, sunset light.
```
