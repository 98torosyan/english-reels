# Mila & Leo — first 10 lessons

Before the first lesson, paste the rules (Mila-Leo-rules.md) in a new ChatGPT chat with Mila's picture. Then send each lesson order below, one at a time.


## 1. Meet Mila and Leo  (53 words)

**Text**

This is Mila. She is nine, and she has red curly hair. She loves drawing. This is her little brother, Leo. He is six, and he loves dinosaurs. They have a fluffy cat. Her name is Toffee. Every day, Mila draws, Leo plays, and Toffee sleeps. 'We are a happy family!' says Leo.

**Order for ChatGPT**

```
Lesson: Meet Mila and Leo. Place: their cosy home in Yerevan. Outfits: Mila N12, Leo L3.
1. Mila waves hello in the sunny living room, her sketchbook under her arm.
2. Close-up: Mila draws a flower in her sketchbook, smiling.
3. Leo roars like a dinosaur, holding a toy dinosaur.
4. Toffee the cat sits on the sofa between cushions.
5. Mila draws, Leo plays on the rug, Toffee sleeps in the sun.
6. Mila, Leo and Toffee together, laughing, warm golden light.
```

**Text**

**Order for ChatGPT**

```
Lesson: The lost mitten. Place: a snowy park in Yerevan. Outfits: Mila N3, Leo L2.
1. Wide view: a white snowy park, Mila and Leo arrive, happy.
2. They throw snowballs and laugh.
3. Leo looks at his bare hand, worried: one red mitten is missing.
4. Mila and Leo look under a snowy tree and behind a bench.
5. Toffee runs through the snow with a red mitten in her mouth.
6. Leo hugs Toffee and puts on his mitten; Mila smiles.
```
