# Pip's Forest Library - a new hero

**Pip** is a small red fox with round gold glasses and a red scarf. He runs a tiny library inside an old oak tree. He is curious, kind and a little clumsy, and he believes that *books are windows to the whole world*. His friends: wise Owl, shy Hedgehog and two little rabbits.

**Text**

Pip is a little fox. He lives in an old oak tree in the forest. Inside the tree, there is a small library. Every morning, Pip opens the green door and says, 'Good morning, books!' Animals come to read: Owl, Hedgehog and two little rabbits. Pip loves his library. 'Books are like windows,' he says. 'You can see the whole world.'

**Text**

At last, they find the book under a big mushroom. It is dry and safe. Hedgehog smiles. 'Thank you, Pip. You are a true friend.'
