ENGLISH READING ROOM — WORD PICTURES

HOW THIS WORKS
I will send you only a number from 1 to 10. Each time, create exactly ONE image for that number, following the rules below and the list for that number. Do not ask questions, do not write any text in the chat — just make the image.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE (always the same)
- ONE image, portrait 3:4, a grid of 3 columns and 4 rows = 12 square panels, separated by thin white gutters of equal width.
- Each panel shows ONE object only, whole and not cut off, centred, filling about 70% of the panel, on a plain soft warm-cream background (#F7F1E6) with a soft shadow under it.
- Style: warm, detailed children's storybook illustration, soft gouache and coloured-pencil texture, gentle warm light, rich cosy colours. The same style, light and background in all 12 panels, so they look like one set.
- The objects go in the exact order of the list: left to right, top to bottom (1-2-3 is the top row, 10-11-12 is the bottom row).
- Absolutely NO text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.
- Friendly and calm; nothing scary.
